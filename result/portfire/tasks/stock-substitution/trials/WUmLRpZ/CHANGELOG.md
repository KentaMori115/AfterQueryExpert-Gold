# changelog

## unreleased

Everything so far. The first version is not tagged yet, because the show json
schema is still moving and anything that parses it would have to move with it.

### the compiler

- cue script language with fire, ripple, fan, chase, play and group
- includes across files, with the loop reported rather than followed
- time of flight compensation from a measured lift table
- pin allocation that spreads a run across the modules at a position
- quantisation onto the panel's own frame, with the drift reported
- the show drawn from the magazine book, lot by lot, with a stand-in of the
  same calibre where the shelf is short and the lot written on every cue

### the checks

- module load against the simultaneous limit each model really has
- density, lulls and the lateral lean of a show
- separation distance under NFPA 1123, the European bands, and reduced
- airspace ceiling, fallout across hard boundaries, wind drift
- noise at the nearest sensitive property
- viewing area capacity and clearance time
- net explosive quantity and hazard division against a store licence

### the advice

- lateral lean and how the show is spread across its quarters
- variety over what effects look like rather than what they are called
- runs worth chaining, with the fuse to cut and the pins it saves
- a second match on the cues that carry weight, and what that costs

### the output

- firing table in three address styles and three time styles
- a lot column on the cue sheet once the show has been drawn from a magazine
- cue sheet, wiring sheet and per position sheets
- rack layout and the tube hire order
- permit facts, transport lines and the whole show pack
- machine readable json with a version on it
