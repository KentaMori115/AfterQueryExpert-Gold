# MainGott Instagram Reel — Creative Brief

## Objective
Advertise MainGott as a technology/business platform, not as a collection of people.

Core idea:
> MainGott connects customer acquisition, AI, CRM, automation, communication channels, sales, payments, and analytics into one intelligent operating system.

## Audience
Business owners, executives, decision makers, and companies interested in digital transformation, AI, CRM, automation, and integrated sales operations.

## Core positioning
**MAINGOTT SALES & OPERATIONS OS**

Supporting idea:
**From first contact to purchase — one connected customer journey.**

## 40-second narrative
1. 0–4s: fragmented channels — `Your customers are everywhere.`
2. 4–8s: lost context — `Lost context. Lost opportunities.`
3. 8–12s: MainGott reveal — `Meet MainGott.` / `SALES & OPERATIONS OS`
4. 12–18s: connected channels and core — `One connected system.`
5. 18–23s: AI intent/qualification — `AI understands the task.`
6. 23–29s: customer journey — `One customer. One context. One journey.`
7. 29–35s: analytics/management — `Every action becomes data.`
8. 35–40s: logo close — `MAINGOTT` / `SALES & OPERATIONS OS`

## Visual language
Preferred:
- dark premium environment
- clean data-flow animation
- restrained futuristic UI
- realistic enterprise interfaces
- cinematic camera movement
- professional B2B aesthetic

Avoid:
- humanoid robots
- random code rain
- cyberpunk clichés
- generic stock-office scenes
- excessive neon
- fake unreadable dashboards

## Audio
Professional, calm, confident voiceover. Modern electronic/cinematic background music with enough space for narration.

## CTA
Use one simple brand CTA only. Do not overload the final frame.
