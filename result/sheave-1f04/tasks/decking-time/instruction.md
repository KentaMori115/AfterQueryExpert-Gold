Sheave takes a winder's standing time as a figure typed into `profile`. In a shallow shaft it is the cycle's biggest item and comes off a landing. Work it out.

Give the cycle namespace `decking({perTub: 6, tubsADeck: 2, pitch: 2.4, settle: 4, discharge: 12})`, refusing a negative, a tub time or pitch of nought, or part of a tub. `deckChange(one)` is a deck's tubs at so many seconds each, `deckMove(one, creep)` the pitch at creep speed plus the keps, `redecking(one, decks, creep)` one move for every deck after the first. `standAt(one, what, creep)` is a cage's decks and moves, a skip's discharge whatever its decks, nothing for a counterweight. `stand(one, rising, falling, creep)` is the slower landing, not the two added: both are worked at once. `withDecking(how, one, rising, falling)` returns the profile standing that long, nothing else touched; `standingShare(how, one, rising, falling, wind)` and `standingADay(how, one, rising, falling, wind, hours)` set it against the cycle and a working day, in hours.

A cage takes one to six decks. `deckPayload(one, decks, aTub)` is the coal they hold, `deckedCage(one, decks, allowed, aTub)` a cage tared by the cage ratio on that coal, filled to `payloadAllowed` when a rope allowance in kilonewtons will not carry it, `decksAllowed(one, allowed, aTub)` the most decks a rope fills. `raises(how, one, decks, wind, allowed, aTub)` and `raisesADay(how, one, decks, wind, allowed, hours, aTub)` are what it wins, `worthOfADeck(how, one, decks, wind, allowed, aTub)` what another deck is worth, `bestDecks(how, one, wind, allowed, most, aTub)` the count raising most, `deckSheet(how, one, wind, allowed, most, aTub)` a row a count with its `decks`, `payload`, `stand`, `cycle` and `raises`. `sheave decking` prints it, taking `--depth`, `--decks`, `--allowed`.
