Rehearsals double-book equipment. When a cue's turn comes while one of its `uses` resources is still reserved, the scheduler starts it anyway and reports CF4001 or CF4002 over the finished timeline. A stage manager would hold the cue until the device frees, and that is what `rehearse` and `report` should do. `compile` keeps flagging such plans as now.

A cue is called at its planned instant: what the compiled plan, delays and GO give it. If every resource it uses has a free slot it starts there, as today. A slot is free while fewer than `capacity` started cues hold the resource; a holder releases at completion or failure, and reservations stay half-open, so a release at instant T frees the slot for a cue called at T. Otherwise the cue holds, holding nothing meanwhile, and starts at the first instant every one of its resources has a free slot; `requires` states are checked then. When several cues could take the last free slot at one instant, earlier planned instant wins, then smaller cue id.

A held cue drags its `after` dependents along. A dependent with offset zero or more is called at the dependency's actual start plus offset, plus its own delay, never before the dependency started; that call is its planned instant. A dependency failing before it starts calls them from the failure instant. Dependents with negative offsets keep the planned instant they already had. Absolute, event and manual cues are never moved by another cue's hold.

Every cue that started later than called gets one CF7001 warning, subject kind `cue`, witness `planned_ms`, `start_ms`, `held_ms` and `resources`: used resource ids without a free slot at the planned instant, sorted, comma joined. Rehearsal result and its JSON carry a `holds` list ordered by planned instant then cue id, entries `cue_id`, `planned_ms`, `start_ms`, `held_ms`, `resources` as a list. Events keep their shape: `eligible` at the planned instant, `started` at the actual one. Holds alone are no errors, so the command exits 0. State the contract in docs/timing-contract.md and note it in CHANGELOG.md.
